Our method

Our approach is built around the proprietary Dual Loop method — a two-step strategy designed to turn opportunities into viable startups.

Deep Dive

This first phase focuses on identifying concrete use cases based on real-world needs. It ensures that each project is grounded in industrial reality, with a validated market potential and a well-understood technological feasibility.

Fast Forge

In this second loop, we transform an idea into a prototype in record time. By leveraging hybrid classical/quantum tools and pilot partners, we accelerate proof of usefulness and drastically shorten incubation cycles.

The McCann Perkins Venture Journey

Weeks 0 to 4

Align – Strategic Framing & Commitment

We begin with a focused diagnostic: founding team, market dynamics, and ambition. We define roles, align on vision, and formalize the engagement (sweat equity, governance, roadmap).

  • Diagnostic of founding team and market context
  • Vision, roles, and governance clarified
  • Engagement pact and equity framework outlined
  • Initial roadmap and milestones defined
Deliverables: Strategic roadmap, equity framework, engagement pact
Months 1 to 3

Build – Structure, Product & Positioning

We co-develop the legal, technical, and business foundations. Together we define the core offer, clarify the positioning, and set up the first operational tools.

  • Legal and business foundations put in place
  • Core offer and value proposition refined
  • Technical architecture and security baseline designed
  • Brand identity and narrative created
Deliverables: Pitch deck, MVP specs, business model, brand identity
Months 4 to 8

Launch – MVP, Clients & First Revenue

We ship a functional MVP, test it with early adopters, and initiate commercial traction or fundraising. McCann Perkins remains deeply involved in day-to-day execution.

  • MVP delivered and tested with early adopters
  • First commercial traction and feedback loops
  • Investor-ready materials prepared
  • Day-to-day execution supported by our team
Deliverables: Working MVP, first leads or clients, investor-ready materials
Months 9 to 12+

Scale or Transition – Traction, Autonomy & Growth

Once momentum is built, we help secure funding or consolidate the team's independence. Our role evolves — we remain as strategic advisors or transition to a board-level partner.

  • Funding or strategic partnerships secured
  • Team reinforced for independence
  • Our role shifts to board-level partner or advisor
  • Long-term governance and strategy maintained
Deliverables: Revenue or capital raised, independent team, board engagement
